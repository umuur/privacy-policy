Thank you for playing Never Blunder (“the Game”). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play the Game.

1. Information We Collect
1.1 Personal Information

Never Blunder does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

1.2 Gameplay Data

The Game may store local gameplay data on your device, such as:

Game progress

Settings and preferences

Unlocks, achievements, or run history

This data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

2. Advertising

Never Blunder does not display third-party ads
OR
Never Blunder may display third-party ads (e.g., on mobile platforms). Ad providers may use anonymized identifiers (such as device IDs) to show relevant ads in accordance with their own privacy policies.

You can opt out of personalized ads through your device’s system settings.

3. In-App Purchases

If the Game offers in-app purchases:

All payments are processed by the platform provider (Apple, Google, Steam, etc.)

We do not receive or store payment or billing information

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Anonymous analytics data may be shared with trusted service providers solely for the purpose of improving the Game.

5. Children’s Privacy

Never Blunder does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any data handled by the Game. Since most data is stored locally on your device, you remain in control of it. Uninstalling the Game will remove all locally stored data.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights under data protection laws such as GDPR or CCPA, including the right to:

Access information about data collection

Request deletion of data (where applicable)

Opt out of analytics or personalized ads

Because Never Blunder does not store personal data, most of these requests can be fulfilled by adjusting your device settings or uninstalling the Game.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the Game after changes means you accept the updated policy.
